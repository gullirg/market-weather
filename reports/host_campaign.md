Two code facts belong in this record.

1. The yuan registration reads "EXCHUS from 2006 only, pre-reform peg
   excluded by construction" but the REGISTRY build did not implement
   the clause. The slice is now enforced by `YUAN_START` in the build.
   `rolling_z` divides by a rolling standard deviation and the 1995 to
   2005 hard peg supplies none, so the clause is load-bearing. The
   change was made and chained as REG-yuan-2006 before the first yuan
   decode. No template, window or check definition was altered.
2. The analogue engine raised `KeyError` the moment a node outside its
   hand-written weight roster entered the panel, which silently dropped
   the site analogues to null on the em_dollar join. The registered
   similarity rule is "oil weight 2, others 1, synoptic 1.5", so
   unlisted nodes now take the registered other-weight. The chained AN1
   result was not rescored.

The activity and housing joins were committed together because the test
suite is green only with both. The activity block is the equal-weight
mean of its available members; with WEI alone that block starts 2009-12
and the block GFEVD common sample fell from 433 to 200 months, failing
`test_sparse_map_structure`. HOUST is the block's other registered
member and runs from 1959, so adding it restored the sample to 433
months. No test threshold was touched.

After all seven, `test_sparse_map_structure` and
`test_network_membership_scored` pass on the enlarged panel. The block
GFEVD runs on 432 months, the sparse map on 22 nodes over 18 windows
with 151 stable edges. Chained as POPULATION-1.

## Stage 2, the G9 remainder feeds

Landed and placed under health monitoring, none flagged: hy_oas, ovx,
ig_oas, claims, mortgage, fed_bs, btc, eu_equities, plus ovx-daily for
Stage 3. `health.MONITORED` holds feeds that are watched and wired to no
decoder, which changes no instrument. `providers.FRED_SERIES` now lists
every series the host fetches. `fred_csv_to_series` is a new parser and
arrived with its own fixture test.

1. claims joined, 3 of 3. Registered spec-first as REG-claims before
   any decode of it: ICSA weekly to monthly mean, feature
   `-100*log(claims)` so up means an improving labour market, level z
   240m min 60 and 3m change z 120m min 36, states calm, expansion and
   contraction with templates copied from `activity`, block activity.
   CJ1 dominant contraction 1.00, CJ2 present 1.00, CJ3 present 1.00.
2. TTF is UNAVAILABLE. Stooq answers every CSV request from this host
   with a bot interstitial rather than data, and FRED carries no TTF
   series. PNGASEUUSDM, the IMF euro-area natural gas price, is a free
   and stable substitute but it is a different series and registering
   it as TTF would be dishonest, so it was not landed.
3. EU equities landed from FRED as SPASTT01EZM661N, satisfying the
   "EU or EM equities" requirement. Its last observation is 2026-01, so
   it lags by seven months and the health monitor reports that.
4. hy_oas and ig_oas landed but only from 2023-08. The free fredgraph
   endpoint clamps the ICE BofA OAS series to a rolling three-year
   window; a control request for VIXCLS over the same range returns
   1990 onward, so the clamp is specific to those series and no FRED
   API key is present on this host. Three years of history cannot
   score a held-out check on 2008, 2015 or 2020, so no instrument was
   registered on either feed. Registering checks known in advance to be
   unscoreable would manufacture a miss rather than test anything.
5. ovx is in health monitoring only. Using it as an oil volatility
   dimension would change a live decoder and no such respecification is
   registered, so the live decoder was not touched.
6. mortgage, fed_bs and btc are feeds only. G9 does not name them as
   nodes and no instrument spec exists for them.

### Hard stop, recorded

G4 was not scored. The instruction was to run it once exactly as
registered, but G4 on the chain is a registered queue entry, status
pending, `auto` false, claim "high-yield OAS feed for sectoral credit
stress", with no window, no target, no mode and no threshold. There is
no registered scoring rule to execute, so any status assigned to it
would be invented rather than measured. G4 stays pending. Resolving it
needs either a registered check definition for the HY OAS instrument or
a FRED API key that lifts the three-year clamp, and probably both.

## Stage 3, G12, the daily clock as its own instrument

Registered as REG-G12 before any estimation: business-day grid,
filtered causal decode, four family-level states (calm, boom, fear,
downturn) with fixed templates over four features (63bd real Brent
momentum, 21bd realized volatility, daily OVX masked before 2007-05,
crude stocks ex-SPR carried forward causally), stay probability 0.995,
nothing fitted. Agreement with the monthly clock was not retried in any
form. One estimation, one scoring.

1. D1 hit. All five pinned episodes captured within 25 business days:
   1990-08 fear, 2008-10 downturn, 2014-11 downturn, 2020-03 downturn,
   2026-03 fear.
2. D2 missed. 32.26 family flips per decade, 114 flips over 8906
   eligible business days, against a registered budget of 6.
3. D3 hit. Median family run length 46.0 business days over 125 runs,
   against a floor of 40.
4. The gate is shut. It required D1 and D2 both to hit. The site gains
   no daily chip and the record page keeps its shadow wording. On this
   architecture the daily clock is a shadow instrument permanently.
   Episode capture is not the binding constraint and persistence is not
   either. The instrument reads the episodes and then chatters between
   them.

No template, stay probability, window or check was adjusted after
scoring. `run_g12` is deliberately not wired into the monthly loop, so
the scored run is not repeated; its result is frozen in
`state/g12_result.json`.

The three checks are registered as D1, D2 and D3 in the daily docstring
and chained as G12-D1, G12-D2 and G12-D3, because ids D1 through D4
were already held by the graph-edges group. Only the chain label is
namespaced; no definition changed.
