A node is one block of the economy, holding the instruments that belong to it.
Colour is the family that block is reading now, from calm through easing, up, strained and hot.
An edge percentage is the share of one block's forecast error variance that another block explains.
Breathing is confidence: the surer the reading, the slower and deeper the pulse.
Every instrument passes registered checks before it joins, and failures publish beside the hits in the record.
