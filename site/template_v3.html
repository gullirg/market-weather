Method, briefly

Sources and lineage

Six economically defined oil regimes plus four smaller instruments, each a fixed-template hidden Markov classifier over standardised fundamentals: prices, volatility, spreads, inventories, a commodity demand factor. State meanings are fixed; only realistic state spreads are estimated. Identification follows Kilian and Kilian-Murphy, connectedness follows Diebold-Yilmaz, validation is pre-registered held-out episode scoring with published failures. Sources: EIA, FRED, Shiller, IMF public series.
